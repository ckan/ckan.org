{% translate "We’re a distributed community of nearly 300 contributors, from all corners of the world and all walks of life, united in our desire to build the best open-source data management system. Come aboard!" %}

If you work with data or Python, the weekly Dev Meetings are a great place to start: open to everyone, no experience required. Drop in to listen, ask questions or find out where your skills are most useful. Ready to dig in? Issues labelled Good for Contribution are a reliable starting point, and the Rockstar Tickets list higher-impact work for a bigger challenge.

{% translate "Need help with CKAN?" %}

Stack Overflow and the CKAN docs cover most common questions. GitHub Discussions is the place for support requests, sharing your project and questions about CKAN, and Gitter is where developers talk in real time. For broader conversations (funding, events, running a portal, community strategy) head to the CKAN Community Forum.

Found a bug or a documentation error? Open an issue on the issue tracker. For security vulnerabilities, email security@ckan.org directly and don’t post them publicly.

In order to work with your MailChimp account and automatically send each contact info to your MailChimp audience/list after form submitting please pay attention for the next necessary steps/notes:

  1. 1. Create the following two additional merged fields in your MailChimp audience: COMPANY, FORM
  2. 2. Use segmentation MailChimp functionality (for example, by FORM field) for contacts groupping
  3. 3. "Success stories audience ID" must be a separate audience: "Get notified of new stories" sign-ups were promised story updates only, no newsletters. New sign-ups are added as "pending" and confirm via Mailchimp's own opt-in email. To sync sign-ups collected before this was set, run python manage.py sync_story_subscribers.
